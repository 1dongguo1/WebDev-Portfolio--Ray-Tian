My Design Thinking Process

1

Building

First to determine a framework for the website, I need to think about the overall structure and layout of the website. And find out what color I am going to use.

2

Define

Based on user research, identifying core problems and design goals. I determined key features to showcase, and find out which projects I am going to show.

3

Ideate

Brainstorming multiple solutions to enhance user experience. I explored various layouts and visual designs to create a more user-friendly website.

4

Adjusting

Ask my friends to give me some feedback and adjust the website based on their suggestions. Such as the color, the layout, the content, etc.

5

Test

Debugging my website using developer tools. Press F12 to open the developer tools and check the website. Add some small and interesting features such as the music bar and back to top button.
