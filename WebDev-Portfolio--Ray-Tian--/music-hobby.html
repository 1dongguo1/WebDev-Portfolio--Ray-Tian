About My Music Journey

Music is a vital part of my life, it can express my emotions and provide a creative outlet. My music hobby reflects my passion for sound and expression. When face some problems, I will listen to music to relax my mind and clear my head.

Main Interests

  • Playing piano when I have free time
  • Learning music production techniques and sound design

Favorite Tools

  • Instruments: Piano, Guitar, etc.

Music Inspirations

My music is inspired by artists like Mozart, Beethoven, and Chopin, those are the most famous classical music composers. I aim to create music that resonates with listeners on a personal level.
